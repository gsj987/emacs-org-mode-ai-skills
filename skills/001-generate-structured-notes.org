** Description
Transform selected Org content into a concise, structured outline.

** Inputs
- org-selection

** Outputs
- structured-org-outline
- output must be the final Org artifact only (no explanations, no thinking trace, no progress narration)
- rewritten content must preserve the original language of the selected section

** Steps
1. Analyze heading and paragraph structure.
2. Propose concise section headers.
3. Return an Org subtree with TODO markers where useful.
